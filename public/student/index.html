    How your multiplier works

    Your points multiplier climbs the more you use WeRewards over the last 30 days. You earn a score from 0–1000 that blends three things:

    • Variety — how many different spots you visit
    • Loyalty — how often you come back to the same spots
    • Spend — how much you spend, with extra weight for meal-sized orders

    Keeping all three balanced climbs fastest. Reach 350 for 1.5× points, or 700 for 2× — the multiplier applies to everything you earn. It's a rolling 30-day window, so keep visiting to hold your tier.

    Community points

    Every time you earn at a WeRewards spot, 10% of those points drop into your community balance too.

    • Earn 150 pts at a spot → 15 community points
    • They're on top of your normal points — your spot balance isn't touched
    • They spend at any spot, not just the one that gave them to you

    To spend them, tap the card once you have some: pick a spot, and they become that spot's points. Heads up — a move can't be undone.

    🫂

    Move community points

    Pick a spot and your community points become that spot's points — redeem them there like any others.

    ⚠️ This can't be undone. Moved points stay at that spot.

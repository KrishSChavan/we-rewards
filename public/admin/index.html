      Business name

      What students and the terminal see. The short id beside it in the roster is fixed and does not follow a rename.

      Location name

      Only for a vendor running more than one location on one login: it is how their staff tell the branches apart in the terminal's store switcher. Leave blank for a single-location vendor. Students never see it.

      Points per dollar

      A $10 purchase at rate 10 earns 100 points. Allowed: 0.5 to 1000.

      What they sell

      Feeds the filter on the students' Spots tab. Up to 3 tags. An untagged spot is not "cheap" — it is simply left out of a filtered view, so it is worth filling in.

      Logo

      Square PNG or JPG. It is shrunk to 128px before it is stored. Students' phones cache the old one for up to an hour, and briefly longer on a phone that has already seen it, so a swap takes a little while to reach everyone.

      Reward items

      Each item needs a point cost, a visit cost, or both. Clear a cost field to drop that way to pay. The switch hides an item from students without deleting it.
